# Combat Camera - Configurable 3.5.0-dev

- Optionally lock onto a camera-facing or closest enemy when combat starts with Smooth or Native tracking. Off by default.
- Fix switching to the nearest enemy after a kill, including enemies that disappear briefly.
- Small camera movements no longer switch away from the enemy who last hit you; you can still aim at another enemy to change targets.
- Fix target selection and camera tracking sometimes stopping during normal play.
- Choose Off, Error, Warning, Info or Debug logging; Warning is the default.
